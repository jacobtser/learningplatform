A small team building for a language that software left behind.

02

What we’re building

A complete learning and translation platform, already running.

The Dangme Learning Platform is a working bilingual learning system for a low-resource Ghanaian language of about 1.4 million speakers. It combines a fine-tuned MarianMT model that translates English ⇄ Dangme, Dangme text-to-speech, 26 learning games, SM-2 spaced-repetition study, a leaderboard and a teacher dashboard with CSV export. Three front-ends — the original Flask app, a Jinja + Tailwind redesign and a Next.js 15 / React 19 single-page app — run over one untouched backend, built offline-first for intermittent connectivity.

03

Why we need credits

The bottleneck is compute and tokens — not ideas, not engineering.

For a low-resource language, the dataset is small — and hand-authoring does not scale.

The bilingual lexicon holds roughly 13,000 Dangme→English and 14,000 English→Dangme entries, plus the sentence pairs in the corpus. That is a real foundation, and still far short of what a language needs.

Authoring curriculum, distractor sets, grammar tables and pronunciation review by hand cannot keep pace with 26 game modes. And our in-app AI tutor runs on free tiers today, so it fails exactly when a learner needs it most.

We are not asking for credits to start. The platform is built and working. We are asking for the compute to take work that already exists and make it scale — and to publish what we learn along the way.

04

What the $1,000 buys

Five workstreams. One quarter of focused, shippable AI work.

The $1,000 credit plan
# Workstream & delivery Credits
01 Curriculum & content generation Graded lesson sets, example sentences and distractor pools generated and reviewed for all 26 game modes — turning a hand-written library into a scalable one. $350
02 Parallel-data expansion & post-editing LLM-assisted back-translation and post-editing to grow the ~13k bilingual lexicon, plus synthetic augmentation for scarce sentence types. $250
03 Translation quality evaluation LLM-as-judge scoring across a held-out EN ⇄ Dangme test set, alongside BLEU and chrF, to find and fix the model’s real failure modes. $150
04 In-app Dangme tutor (beta) Keep the provider-agnostic conversational tutor online for a pilot cohort — no more free-tier outages mid-lesson. $150
05 Speech data prep & pronunciation QA Transcription, tone-mark checking and pronunciation QA for the text-to-speech and pronunciation features. $100
Total One quarter of focused, shippable AI work. $1,000

Provider-agnostic by design. The tutor endpoint already speaks to multiple providers, so whichever company grants the credits, the work runs on their models. And what we build with them — an expanded corpus, grammar tables and evaluation reports — will be published openly, for this language and for the ones that follow.

05

Milestones

Concrete deliverables across twelve months.

0–3

Months · the credit quarter

  • Generate and review graded lesson sets and distractor pools for all 26 game modes.
  • Back-translate and post-edit to grow the ~13k bilingual lexicon.
  • Stand up LLM-as-judge evaluation on a held-out EN ⇄ Dangme test set, alongside BLEU and chrF.
  • Keep the in-app Dangme tutor online for a pilot cohort.

3–6

Months · depth & speech

  • Add synthetic augmentation for scarce sentence types and grammar patterns.
  • Prepare and QA speech data feeding the text-to-speech and pronunciation features.
  • Publish the first open release of the expanded corpus and grammar tables.

6–12

Months · openness & reach

  • Publish the translation-quality evaluation report openly.
  • Extend the approach to other low-resource Ghanaian languages.
  • Harden the tutor and content pipeline for classroom use.
